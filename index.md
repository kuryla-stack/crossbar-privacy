Crossbar privacy policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Crossbar is an offline football skill game. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

Information the app stores

Crossbar keeps a few numbers on this phone so the game can remember you between sessions: whether you finished the introduction, whether haptics are on, your best score, your best streak, how many shots and crossbars you have played, and the scores of your latest rounds. These values live in the app's private storage. They are not an account and they are not tied to your name.

What the app does not do

Crossbar does not ask you to sign in. It does not request contacts, photos, location, or the advertising identifier. It does not contain ads. It does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools. It does not send the records above to a server, because the game does not talk to a server at all.

Sharing

Crossbar does not sell, rent, or share personal information. There is no personal information collected to share.

Children

The game is written for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new text will ship with an updated version of the app. Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer address shown on the store listing.
